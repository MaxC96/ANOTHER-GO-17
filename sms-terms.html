GSG Meeting Notifications

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Program Name and Description

GSG Meeting Notifications is an internal SMS program operated by Gotham Solutions Group. It sends low-volume, non-marketing reminders to participating Gotham Solutions Group personnel about upcoming Outlook calendar meetings and scheduled internal appointments. Messages may include a meeting title, scheduled time, and a short notice that the meeting is approaching.

Consent

Participants enroll by providing their mobile phone number and expressly agreeing to receive automated meeting and calendar reminder text messages from Gotham Solutions Group. Participation is voluntary. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies based on the participant's meeting schedule and reminder settings. Message and data rates may apply. Your wireless carrier's standard messaging and data charges may apply to messages sent to or from the program.

Opt Out and Help

STOP: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from GSG Meeting Notifications. After an opt-out request is processed, you will no longer receive program messages unless you later opt in again.

HELP: Reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact Gotham Solutions Group at info@gothamsolutionsgroup.com or (212) 542-1300.

Delivery

Message delivery is subject to network availability and carrier processing. Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Information collected through this program is handled in accordance with the GSG Meeting Notifications Privacy Policy. Gotham Solutions Group does not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Program Changes or Termination

Gotham Solutions Group may modify, suspend, or discontinue the GSG Meeting Notifications program or these terms as operational or compliance requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For questions regarding GSG Meeting Notifications, contact Gotham Solutions Group at info@gothamsolutionsgroup.com or (212) 542-1300.
