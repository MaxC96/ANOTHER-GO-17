Why Gotham

Curiosity leads to discovery.

Gotham was founded in 1997 with a simple idea: the best opportunities are often the ones others overlook.

Built on industry expertise and a consultant's mindset, Gotham began with one goal: helping organizations solve complex problems and uncover opportunities others had missed. From the beginning, our approach has been rooted in asking better questions, challenging assumptions, and finding smarter, more efficient ways to work.

Our story began with our founder, Danya. After years in healthcare consulting, she set out to build a different kind of firm—one that delivered exceptional client outcomes while creating meaningful careers that supported families. What started as a small consulting practice quickly earned a reputation for solving the kinds of problems others overlooked.

As clients experienced measurable results, Gotham grew through word of mouth from healthcare and nonprofit organizations into government, finance, legal, manufacturing, private equity, and beyond. Along the way, Gotham recovered millions of dollars for clients, pioneered innovative technology solutions, and became the independent advocate organizations trusted when the stakes were highest.

Today, nearly three decades later, Gotham continues to be guided by the same principle that inspired its founding: curiosity leads to discovery. Whether recovering costs, optimizing technology, or solving complex operational challenges, we help organizations uncover opportunities others don't see and deliver lasting value for the people they serve.
