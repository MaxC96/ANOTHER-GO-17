Privacy Policy

GSG Meeting Notifications

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GSG Meeting Notifications SMS program operated by Gotham Solutions Group. The program is used only to send low-volume, non-marketing meeting and calendar reminders to Gotham Solutions Group personnel who have agreed to receive them.

Information We Collect

For this SMS program, Gotham Solutions Group may collect and process information such as your name, mobile phone number, opt-in or opt-out status, message delivery status, and limited calendar information needed to create a reminder, such as a meeting title, date, and time.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to administer the GSG Meeting Notifications program, send requested meeting and calendar reminders, manage consent preferences, provide support, maintain program security, and comply with applicable messaging requirements.

SMS Data and Consent

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers, SMS consent, and opt-in records are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process limited information on our behalf only as necessary to operate the program, such as providing calendar, messaging, hosting, security, or telecommunications services. They are not authorized to use SMS opt-in information for their own marketing.

Retention and Security

We retain SMS program information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the program, document consent, resolve support issues, and meet legal or compliance obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect this information.

Your Choices

You may opt out of GSG Meeting Notifications at any time by replying STOP to a program message. For assistance, reply HELP, email info@gothamsolutionsgroup.com, or call (212) 542-1300.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the SMS program or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown above identifies the current version.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or the GSG Meeting Notifications program may be directed to Gotham Solutions Group at info@gothamsolutionsgroup.com or (212) 542-1300.
